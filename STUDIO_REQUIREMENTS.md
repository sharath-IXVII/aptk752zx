# GetYr Studio - requirements (from the channel owner)

These rules are given to the lesson writer AI and the reviewer AI for EVERY video.

## Teaching
1. Viewers are complete beginners who know nothing. Explain every concept in depth: how it ACTUALLY works,
   step by step, with the "why" behind each step (e.g. a filter: show each cycle and how it converts).
2. Show, don't just tell. Every scene's animation must show EXACTLY what the narration is saying at that
   moment. If the narration talks about a part, a step, a menu or a number, that thing is on screen.
3. Explaining alone does not solve it - students must SEE it: real photos, circuits with electron flow,
   oscilloscope waves, app-screen clicks, installer pages, diagrams.
4. Anything to download, install or set up is shown step by step (website, installer pages, clicks,
   commands) and each step is explained: what it does and why.
5. Title cards and text lists are not visuals. Use them only for the opening and the recap.
6. Videos are long and detailed (5-10 minutes). Never summarise the script into tiny scenes.
7. No questions / quiz for now.
8. 100% accurate: every fact, number, formula, direction of current, command and menu path is correct.

## Look and sound
9. No captions/subtitles on the screen.
10. Our own templates (not NotebookLM): light themes per channel, our own animations.
11. Voice: Kokoro "af_heart", natural pace. No "pause and think / three, two, one".
12. Animations are a bit fast and lively, never slow.

## Thumbnails and Shorts
13. Video thumbnail: clean bold title (keyword in colour) + a real frame of the video's most visual scene.
14. Short: the WHOLE concept in 50-60 seconds (hook -> what -> how, step by step -> takeaway), its first
    frame shows the title AND the real visual, and it ends with
    "For the detailed explanation, watch the full video on our channel."

## Process
15. Perfect one channel (ECE) first, with private test videos, then move the others.
